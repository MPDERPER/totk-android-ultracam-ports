UltraCam 2.7.1 predates the 3.0.0 Calamity rewrite and has a narrower historical compatibility context. Although I report successful Android gameplay through 1.4.2 with both packages, that does not convert 2.7.1 into officially supported upstream coverage for every listed version.

Do not obtain game updates from unauthorized sources. Compatibility can change with future emulator and UltraCam builds.
